## Full description (4000)

Shed your tail. Lock your prey.

Shedlock is Snake, reinvented. Eat to grow, and the longer you are the more every bite is worth. But you can do something no snake could before: shed your tail. Everything behind your head becomes walls for ten seconds. Use them to block your own path, cut the board in two, or corner prey that runs away from you.

TRAP RUNNERS
Some food doesn't sit still. Runners flee when you get close. Box one in with your body and your shed walls until it has nowhere to go, and you lock it for a big bonus.

REWIND THREE SECONDS
Crashed? Rewind to three seconds before and try a different move. One free rewind every game.

ONE DAILY BOARD FOR EVERYONE
A new Daily Challenge every day, with the same board, obstacles and food for every player in the world. You get one ranked attempt. Share your score as an emoji grid and keep your streak going.

ENDLESS MODE
Play as long as you like on a fresh board every time and chase your best score.

MAKE IT YOURS
Clean light and dark looks that follow your phone, extra themes (Sage, Sand, Ink), and a full Retro LCD mode in five classic colours. Snake skins and trails too. Everything is cosmetic: there's nothing to buy that helps you win.

FAIR AND FRIENDLY
- Free to play, with optional rewarded ads you choose to watch
- Remove Ads with a one-time purchase
- No account, no sign-in, and your progress stays on your device
- Smooth swipe controls, haptics and crisp sound effects
- Plays offline

Swipe to turn. Tap to shed. Lock your prey.
